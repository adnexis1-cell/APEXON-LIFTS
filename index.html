Every Apexon elevator comes equipped with redundant electro-mechanical safety systems to ensure complete peace of mind.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if there is a power cut while inside an Apexon lift?

All Apexon elevators come standard with an ARD (Automatic Rescue Device) battery backup system. Within seconds of power failure, the lift uses its internal emergency inverter to smoothly travel to the nearest floor, open its doors automatically, and safely release all passengers.

Can a home lift be installed in an already constructed house without a pit?

Yes, absolutely. We provide specialized hydraulic and self-supporting glass capsule lifts that require zero civil pit (or just a shallow 100mm pit) and minimal headroom. They can be installed inside a stairwell cut-out or attached to the external facade of your home.

How quickly does your breakdown maintenance team respond?

We maintain dedicated service crews stationed across Farrukhabad, Fatehgarh, Shahjahanpur, and Firozabad. For emergency passenger entrapper calls, our response time is under 30 minutes, available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

How long does it take from order placement to final lift commissioning?

Standard residential and commercial lifts typically take between 3 to 5 weeks for custom manufacturing and civil shaft preparation, followed by 7 to 10 days of precision mechanical alignment, electrical wiring, and multi-point safety testing.
